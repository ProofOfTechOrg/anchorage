A deferred schedule fire that flowsafe 0.26.0 or earlier stored with a target nested more than 256 levels deep is resolved from the `status` lookup. Before this change the tick recorded every such fire as `failed` with reason `invalid-deferred-dispatch` without asking its target, even when its run had started. The tick now records the target's answer: `published` with reason `dispatch-reconciled` for a run the target holds, the receipt's outcome for a threaded signal whose receipt the target stored, or `failed` with reason `start-error-confirmed` when the lookup states that the target holds no such run. It never sends such a dispatch again, and a lookup that throws still settles the fire as `failed` with reason `invalid-deferred-dispatch`, recording the lookup's error as `statusError`. The settled trigger keeps the dispatch's identifiers without its target. A custom `ScheduleTickStatus` seam is now also called with such a dispatch, whose `workflowTarget` or `agentTarget` holds the deep value. Flowsafe 0.26.1 and 0.26.2 settle these fires on their first reconcile pass, so the change reaches deployments that upgrade from 0.26.0 or earlier, and fires an earlier version writes during a gradual deploy or rollback.

A run or thread object's read of the schedule fire it is asked to execute finds no fire when the fire's record is one SQLite's JSON functions cannot parse, and the object refuses the start or signal as it refuses a fire it cannot find. Before this change the read failed with `malformed JSON` and the request answered with an internal error.
